## Operating Leverage Analysis: Microsoft FY2023–FY2025

**Bottom line: Microsoft delivered positive operating leverage in all three years — operating income grew faster than revenue every year — but the size of that advantage is fading, not accelerating.**

### Data (Consolidated, GAAP, $ millions)

| Fiscal Year | Revenue | Operating Income | Op. Margin | Revenue YoY | Op. Income YoY | Leverage Gap (OI% − Rev%) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| FY2023 | $211,915 | $88,523 | 41.8% | +7%¹ | +6%¹ | ~‑1 pt |
| FY2024 | $245,122 | $109,433 | 44.6% | +15.7% | +23.6% | +7.9 pts |
| FY2025 | $281,724 | $128,528 | 45.6% | +14.9% | +17.5% | +2.5 pts |

¹FY2023 growth rates per company-reported figures in the FY23 10-K (source below).
