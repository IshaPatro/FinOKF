# turn-002

## Question

What should a portfolio manager take away from Microsoft's consolidated operating leverage across FY2023, FY2024 and FY2025? Was operating profit outpacing revenue, and was that advantage accelerating or fading?

## Answer

Conclusion: Microsoft's consolidated operating leverage has been increasing across FY2023, FY2024, and FY2025, with operating margins expanding by 287.1 and 97.8 basis points, respectively.

Drivers: The increase in operating leverage can be attributed to the company's ability to maintain a high operating margin despite revenue growth. The incremental operating margin, which measures the change in operating margin as a percentage of revenue, has been relatively stable at around 50-60% over the past two years.

Quantitative Comparison:

| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| FY2023 | 211,915.000 | 88,523.000 | 41.77% |
| FY2024 | 245,122.000 | 109,433.000 | 44.64% |
| FY2025 | 281,724.000 | 128,528.000 | 45.62% |

| Fiscal Year (Exact Period) | ΔRevenue (USD millions) | ΔOperating Income (USD millions) | ΔOperating Margin (%) |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| FY2023 → FY2024 | 33,207.000 | 20,910.000 | 62.97% |
| FY2024 → FY2025 | 36,602.000 | 19,095.000 | 52.17% |

The increase in operating leverage is driven by the company's ability to maintain a high operating margin despite revenue growth. The incremental operating margin has been relatively stable, indicating that the company's operating efficiency has improved over time.

Limitations: The analysis is based on reported-period calculations and does not establish the cause or future persistence of the change in operating leverage. Additionally, the data only provides a snapshot of the company's financial performance and does not account for other factors that may influence operating leverage, such as changes in business mix or operating expenses.
