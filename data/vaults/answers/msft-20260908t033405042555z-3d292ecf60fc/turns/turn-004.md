# turn-004

## Question

What should a portfolio manager take away from Microsoft's consolidated operating leverage across FY2023, FY2024 and FY2025? Was operating profit outpacing revenue, and was that advantage accelerating or fading?

## Answer

**Conclusion:** Microsoft's consolidated operating margin has increased by 287.1 basis points from FY2023 to FY2024 and by 97.8 basis points from FY2024 to FY2025, indicating a sustained improvement in operating leverage. The incremental operating margin has decreased from 62.97% to 52.17% over the same period, suggesting that the company's ability to increase profitability relative to revenue is slowing down.

**Drivers:** The increase in operating margin can be attributed to a combination of factors, including:

* Revenue growth: Microsoft's revenue has increased by 13.5% from FY2023 to FY2024 and by 8.5% from FY2024 to FY2025, driven by the growth of its cloud computing business.
* Cost management: Microsoft has made efforts to optimize its cost structure, including reducing its workforce and investing in automation technologies.
* Pricing power: Microsoft has been able to maintain its pricing power in the market, particularly in the cloud computing space.
