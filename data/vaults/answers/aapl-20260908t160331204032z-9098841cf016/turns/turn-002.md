# turn-002

## Question

How would you characterize Apple's consolidated gross margin performance in FY2023, FY2024 and FY2025: steady progress or an uneven improvement? Explain its significance for an analyst and the limits of extrapolating the trend.

## Answer

Conclusion — Uneven improvement. Apple’s consolidated gross margin widened materially from 44.13% in FY2023 to 46.21% in FY2024 (+207.5 bps) and then increased by a smaller +69.9 bps to 46.91% in FY2025; overall improvement but not a steady, linear progression. (Reported-period calculations) [filings/AAPL/AAPL-FY2025-10-K-2025-10-31-0000320193-25-000079.md].

Key facts (reported)
- FY2023 (2022-09-25 to 2023-09-30): Revenue 383,285; Cost of revenue 214,137; Gross margin 44.13% [USD millions].
- FY2024 (2023-10-01 to 2024-09-28): Revenue 391,035; Cost 210,352; Gross margin 46.21% [USD millions].
- FY2025 (2024-09-29 to 2025-09-27): Revenue 416,161; Cost 220,960; Gross margin 46.91% [USD millions].

Calculations (from supplied data)
- FY23→FY24 = 46.21% − 44.13% = +2.075 percentage points = +207.5 basis points.
- FY24→FY25 = 46.91% − 46.21% = +0.699 percentage points = +69.9 basis points.

Significance for an analyst
- Margin expansion supports higher operating leverage and potential EPS upside if sustained; the big step in FY24 drove most of the improvement.
Limits of extrapolation
- These ratios do not identify causes (product mix, services, cost declines, FX, one‑offs). The smaller FY25 gain suggests the pace slowed; you cannot assume the same step-change will recur without segment-level and non‑GAAP adjustment analysis.
