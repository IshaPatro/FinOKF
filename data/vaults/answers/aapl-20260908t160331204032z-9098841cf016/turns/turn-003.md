# turn-003

## Question

Did Apple's consolidated gross margin change between FY2024 and FY2025 materially improve the economics of each revenue dollar? What does that comparison tell an investor, and what does it leave unresolved?

## Answer

Conclusion — Yes. Apple’s consolidated gross margin rose 69.9 basis points from FY2024 to FY2025, meaning each revenue dollar produced modestly more gross profit, but this change alone does not explain why or whether it will persist.

Reported facts (GAAP, consolidated, USD millions)
- FY2024 revenue 391,035; cost of revenue 210,352; gross margin 46.21% (filing).
- FY2025 revenue 416,161; cost of revenue 220,960; gross margin 46.91% (filing).
(Source below)

My calculations
- Gross profit FY2024 = 391,035 − 210,352 = 180,683 USD mn.
- Gross profit FY2025 = 416,161 − 220,960 = 195,201 USD mn.
- Change = +14,518 USD mn gross profit; revenue up +25,126 USD mn; cost of revenue up +10,608 USD mn.
- Margin change = 46.91% − 46.21% = +0.699 percentage points = +69.9 bps → per $1 revenue: $0.4621 → $0.4691 (+$0.0070 per $1; +$7.0 per $1,000).

Inference for investors
- Positive: marginally improved economics per revenue dollar can lift operating profit and FCF if operating expenses/cash conversion remain stable.
- Unresolved: whether improvement is from sustainable drivers (product mix shift to higher‑margin Services, pricing, cost improvements, scale) or transient factors (one‑time items, timing, FX, accounting/timing differences). It does not measure cash quality (CFO vs NI), capex impact, segment repeatability, or future margin trajectory.
