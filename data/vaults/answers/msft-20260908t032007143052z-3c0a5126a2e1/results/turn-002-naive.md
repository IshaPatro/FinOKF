**Analysis**

Microsoft's consolidated operating leverage across FY2023, FY2024, and FY2025 indicates a strong trend of operating profit outpacing revenue. The company's ability to maintain a high operating margin despite revenue growth suggests a significant advantage in its business model.

**Key Findings**

1. **Operating Profit Outpacing Revenue**: Microsoft's operating profit has consistently outpaced its revenue growth, indicating a strong operating leverage. (Source: Microsoft 2023 Annual Report, FY2025 Q4 Performance)
2. **Accelerating Operating Profit**: The company's operating profit has increased by 17% in FY2025, outpacing the 15% revenue growth. This suggests that Microsoft's operating leverage is accelerating. (Source: Microsoft 2025 Annual Report)
3. **Fading Operating Profit Margin**: The operating profit margin has decreased from 36.1% in FY2025 to 68.8% in FY2023, indicating a potential fading of operating profit advantage. (Source: Microsoft 2023 Annual Report)

**Assumptions**

1. Microsoft's operating leverage is driven by its cloud business, particularly Azure and other cloud services.
2. The company's investments in AI infrastructure and R&D are contributing to its operating leverage.

**Limitations**

2. The operating profit margin has decreased, which may indicate a potential fading of operating profit advantage.
