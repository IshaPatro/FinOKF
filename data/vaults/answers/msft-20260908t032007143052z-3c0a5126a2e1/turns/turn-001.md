# turn-001

## Question

How did the profitability of Microsoft's growth change across FY2023, FY2024 and FY2025? Assess consolidated operating leverage and whether the profitability of additional revenue strengthened or weakened.

## Answer

**Conclusion:** Microsoft's consolidated operating margin increased by 287.1 basis points from FY2023 to FY2024, and by 97.8 basis points from FY2024 to FY2025. The incremental operating margin, which measures the change in operating margin relative to revenue growth, decreased from 62.97% to 52.17% over the same period.

**Drivers and Quantifications:**

* Revenue growth: FY2023 to FY2024: $33,207.000 million; FY2024 to FY2025: $36,602.000 million.
* Operating income growth: FY2023 to FY2024: $20,910.000 million; FY2024 to FY2025: $19,095.000 million.
* Operating margin change: FY2023 to FY2024: +287.1 basis points; FY2024 to FY2025: +97.8 basis points.

**Comparison:**

The incremental operating margin decreased from 62.97% to 52.17% over the two-year period. This suggests that the profitability of additional revenue growth may have weakened.

**Limitations:**

* The analysis is based on reported-period calculations and does not establish the cause or future persistence of the change.
* The incremental operating margin is a snapshot of the company's profitability at a given point in time and may not reflect the underlying drivers of the change.
