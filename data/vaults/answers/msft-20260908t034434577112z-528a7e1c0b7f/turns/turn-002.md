# turn-002

## Question

What should a portfolio manager take away from Microsoft's consolidated operating leverage across FY2023, FY2024 and FY2025? Was operating profit outpacing revenue, and was that advantage accelerating or fading?

## Answer

Conclusion — Microsoft’s operating profit did outpace revenue in FY2023→FY2024 and FY2024→FY2025, but the incremental advantage slowed: high positive operating leverage persisted, yet acceleration faded in FY2025 versus FY2024.

Reported facts (from filings)
- FY2023 revenue $211,915M; operating income $88,523M; operating margin 41.77%.
- FY2024 revenue $245,122M; operating income $109,433M; operating margin 44.64%.
- FY2025 revenue $281,724M; operating income $128,528M; operating margin 45.62%.
(Source: MSFT FY2025 10‑K)

Calculations
- FY23→FY24: revenue +$33,207M (+15.7%); operating income +$20,910M (+23.6%); incremental operating margin = 20,910 / 33,207 = 62.97%. Operating margin change = +287.1 bps.
- FY24→FY25: revenue +$36,602M (+14.9%); operating income +$19,095M (+17.4%); incremental operating margin = 19,095 / 36,602 = 52.17%. Operating margin change = +97.8 bps.

Inference (what this implies and cannot establish)
- Implication: Microsoft realized strong operating leverage each year (incremental OM >> headline margins), so operating profit rose faster than revenue. The incremental return on additional revenue fell from ~63% to ~52%, so the margin expansion is decelerating.
- Limitation: These are reported‑period calculations; they do not establish causes (segment mix, cost changes, one‑offs) or guarantee persistence.
