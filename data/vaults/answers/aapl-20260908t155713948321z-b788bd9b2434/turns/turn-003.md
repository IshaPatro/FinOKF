## Question

Did Apple's consolidated gross margin change between FY2024 and FY2025 materially improve the economics of each revenue dollar? What does that comparison tell an investor, and what does it leave unresolved?

## Answer

Conclusion: Apple's consolidated gross margin increased by 69.9 basis points between FY2024 and FY2025, indicating a material improvement in the economics of each revenue dollar.

Drivers: The increase in gross margin can be attributed to various factors, including cost management, pricing power, and operational efficiency. The company's ability to maintain its market share and pricing power in a competitive market may have contributed to the increase in gross margin.

Quantitative Comparison: The gross margin ratio for FY2025 (46.91%) is higher than that of FY2024 (46.21%). This indicates that Apple was able to maintain its pricing power and control costs, resulting in a higher gross margin.

| Fiscal Year (Exact Period) | Gross Margin Ratio |
| --- | ---: |
| FY2024 | 46.21% |
| FY2025 | 46.91% |

Calculation: (Gross Margin Ratio FY2025 - Gross Margin Ratio FY2024) / Gross Margin Ratio FY2024 × 100 = 69.9 basis points.

This increase in gross margin ratio implies that Apple's revenue dollars generated more profit in FY2025 compared to FY2024.

Implications for Investors: The improvement in gross margin ratio suggests that Apple's business model remains strong, and the company is able to maintain its pricing power and control costs. This is a positive sign for investors, as it indicates that Apple's revenue growth is likely to be driven by increasing profitability.

Limitations: This analysis does not establish the cause of the increase in gross margin ratio. It is possible that the increase is due to various factors, such as changes in cost structure, pricing power, or market conditions. Further analysis is required to determine the underlying drivers of the increase in gross margin ratio.
