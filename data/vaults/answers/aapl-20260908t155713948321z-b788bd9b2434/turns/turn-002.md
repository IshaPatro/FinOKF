# turn-002

## Question

How would you characterize Apple's consolidated gross margin performance in FY2023, FY2024 and FY2025: steady progress or an uneven improvement? Explain its significance for an analyst and the limits of extrapolating the trend.

## Answer

Conclusion: Apple's consolidated gross margin has shown uneven improvement over the past three fiscal years, with a steady increase in FY2023 and a more modest gain in FY2024 and FY2025.

Drivers: The gross margin improvement can be attributed to the company's focus on product mix, pricing strategy, and operational efficiency. The increase in gross margin in FY2023 can be attributed to the higher revenue from the iPhone, while the modest gain in FY2024 and FY2025 may be due to the continued growth of other product segments, such as services and wearables.

Quantitative Comparison:

| Fiscal Year | Revenue | Cost of Revenue | Gross Margin |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| FY2023 | $383,285M | $214,137M | 44.13% |
| FY2024 | $391,035M | $210,352M | 46.21% |
| FY2025 | $416,161M | $220,960M | 46.91% |

Comparison to previous year:

* FY2023 to FY2024: Gross Margin increased by 207.5 basis points (2.2 percentage points)
* FY2024 to FY2025: Gross Margin increased by 69.9 basis points (0.7 percentage points)

Limitations: The analysis is limited by the availability of data and the potential for one-time or seasonal factors to impact the gross margin. Additionally, the gross margin calculation does not account for other factors that may influence the company's profitability, such as operating expenses and tax rates.
