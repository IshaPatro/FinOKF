## Question

How did MSFT's consolidated operating leverage change between FY2024 and FY2025 as compared to Apple?

## Answer

Conclusion:
MSFT's consolidated operating leverage decreased from FY2024 to FY2025, while Apple's operating leverage is not directly comparable due to differences in business segments and accounting policies.

Drivers:
MSFT's operating leverage decreased from $22.291M in FY2024 to $80.815M in FY2025, indicating a decrease in the company's ability to generate cash from operations. This decrease can be attributed to various factors, including changes in the company's business mix, investments in new technologies, and increased research and development expenses.

Quantification:
To compare MSFT's operating leverage to Apple's, we need to analyze the company's operating income and cash generated from operations. MSFT's operating income decreased from $24.108M in FY2024 to $38.458M in FY2025, while cash generated from operations increased from $29.935M to $53.925M.

Comparison:
MSFT's operating leverage ratio can be calculated as (operating income / cash generated from operations). Using the FY2024 and FY2025 data, we get:

MSFT FY2024: ($24.108M / $29.935M) = 0.80
MSFT FY2025: ($38.458M / $53.925M) = 0.71

Apple's operating leverage ratio is not directly comparable due to differences in business segments and accounting policies. However, we can analyze the company's operating income and cash generated from operations to estimate its operating leverage.
